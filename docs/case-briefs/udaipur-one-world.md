## Stage 2 — Strategy

**Question:** What does a company owe the partners who sell its products, beyond a good trip?

**Tension:** Distributors are paid to sell. The event can only be a reward, or it can make them feel part of the brand. A reward ends at the hotel. Belonging has to be designed.

**Principle (proposed):** Belief becomes excellence only when partners share it.

**Behaviour:** The event should behave like a shared belief, built step by step. Each day asks for a small commitment. The "7 Vows" give the community a language. The gala turns the brand's family into the story. The experience moves from invitation to vow, not from arrival to party.

Test: if the seven vows, the daily team challenges and the baraat were removed, would the idea be weaker? Yes. They are the mechanism that turns a trip into shared belief.

## Stage 4 — Draft copy (for review)

**Title:** Udaipur x Tata Motors One World
**Discipline:** Experience Strategy
**Question:** What does a company owe the partners who sell its products, beyond a good trip?

**Opening**
Forty-five countries. Three hundred and fifty partners. One brand, asked to be a family for three days.

**Tension**
A reward ends when the trip does. Partners are paid to sell, and a good hotel does not make them feel part of the brand. Belonging has to be designed, one moment at a time.

**Decision**
We made the partners the team. Each day gave them a shared challenge: kites built around a belief, a fashion show on a fictional budget, a sword to recover together. No one was an audience.

**Decision**
We made the gala a family occasion. The evening opens with a baraat at the hotel forecourt, not in the ballroom, and the brand becomes the family being celebrated.

**Close**
The event ended with seven vows, not a farewell: belief, commitment, collaboration, responsibility, growth and celebration. Each one asked for something that partners could carry home.

**Principle**
Belief becomes excellence only when the people who sell the product share it.
